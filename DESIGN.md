# SR Dental demo direction

Palette: porcelain #ffffff, ice #edf6fb, sky #d6eaf5, clinic blue #145f9c, ink #18384c, slate #5e7280.

Type: locally hosted Manrope. Large, closely spaced medium-weight headings; calm, readable body text.

Layout: a full-width blue editorial hero pairing a left-aligned headline with the clinic's actual doctors; a slim information rail; photographic service cards; an asymmetric team section; reviews; clinic gallery; FAQ and visit details; oversized blue wordmark footer.

```text
logo              navigation                appointment
headline + actions                 actual team photograph
rating              specialties               location
service heading                four photographic cards
team photograph                doctors + approach
review heading                 two real patient reviews
clinic gallery                 visit / FAQ
appointment invitation         contact details
                 SR DENTAL
```

Review against reference: retain generous white space, photographic cards, paired headings and oversized footer. Replace generic medical-stock imagery and invented statistics with actual SR Dental photos, two named specialists, verified hours and Google rating. Blue and white follow the user's chosen direction. No invented doctor portraits, testimonials or treatment outcomes.

Interactions: mobile navigation, treatment details, appointment request preview with click-to-call confirmation, gallery lightbox, FAQ disclosure, map, phone and Instagram links. Booking does not claim to send or confirm an appointment.
