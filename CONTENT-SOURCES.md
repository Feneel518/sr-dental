## Demo copy and behaviour

Headlines and descriptive marketing copy are written for this concept. There are no invented patient counts, experience claims, pricing, guarantees or testimonials. Treatment summaries are consultation invitations, not clinical advice.

The booking form only prepares a request locally in React state. It sends nothing, persists no personal information, and clearly directs visitors to call the clinic to confirm. Copy request writes only when explicitly clicked. Links to telephone, maps, Google reviews and Instagram open their real destinations.

The map uses a location query; confirm its exact pin before launch. The demo is marked noindex and nofollow. A production booking integration and a privacy notice should be added if personal data will be collected or transmitted.
